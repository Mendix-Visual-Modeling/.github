## What Is Mendix

Mendix is a low-code platform for building and running enterprise apps. Searches such as Mendix low-code platform, Mendix app development, Mendix visual modeling, and Mendix microflows usually mean the same goal: ship real apps without a full engineering team.

Mendix keeps the process simple. Create a project, model pages, connect data, and preview in the browser. The Mendix low-code platform approach favors readable panels, predictable menus, and a steady preview loop for business teams, developers, students, and agencies.

In longer sessions, Mendix connects design and logic. Users move from layout to microflows, then to a finished app without switching tools. That matters when a prototype grows into a real workflow and needs integrations and roles.

Mendix also fits common expectations. People checking Mendix app development or Mendix visual modeling usually want stable previews, clear errors, and a calm path from first launch to a deployable result on everyday hardware.
